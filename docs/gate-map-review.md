# WISH WELL gate-map preview — September 29, 2026

This revision adds a minimal 13-gate product map and bottom-up conversation path. It follows Rae’s WISH WELL interpretation, not a historical Tree of Life diagram. Node geometry and the order of conversations are provisional pending her drawing; House identifiers remain distinct from day numbers.

Day order: 6 Intention/Pride; 4 Base; 5 Tasks; 13 Routine/Earth; 11 Wishes; 3 Goals; 1 Projects; 2 Deals; 8 Outcomes; 9 Plan; 10 Backlog; 7 Engine; 12 Dream. Gates 12 and 6 have dashed external connections. A person may visit any gate in any order.

Gate 6 invites relationships, skills, support and team; trauma disclosure is optional and never required. Gate 4 begins with an existing base, including the Google-to-home-server pathway without assuming a connection. Gate 13 combines 5/4/6 as routine. Gate 9 preserves one Goal, eight Outcome spaces and 64 Task spaces; North Node outcomes/retirement and South Node documents/foundation/mission. The Dream holds the whole picture; Wishes supply stories and data; Goals are central.

Opening a gate marks 15%, submitting words marks 60%, and the person explicitly reviewing their reflection marks 100%. These are UI participation/review states, not a readiness score or verified service activation. Editing reopens the gate. Words and history persist per browser and user/preview scope; no cross-device sync. Prior 14-day data is retained separately, never erased or silently remapped. New gate histories also appear in Story.

The shared dialogue supplies internal SOAP and SMART guidance. It does not yet generate or link structured backend work records, prove a ten-minute conversation, or execute work. Do not claim automatic document creation or completed plans. Unknown outcomes/tasks stay empty.

Space is an 8×8 checkerboard with sixteen slots each for Houses, Planets, Dinosaurs and Connections. Twelve actual Houses plus four unassigned spaces occupy rows 1–2. Sixteen standing planet destinations occupy rows 3–4. Twelve existing Dinosaurs plus open spaces occupy rows 5–6. Remaining entities occupy rows 7–8; any overflow appears in a disclosure, not discarded. Gold Queen and assignment controls retained. No new Houses invented to fill squares.

Claude’s Time wheel implementation is not edited by this revision. This remains a preview branch; production has not been merged.

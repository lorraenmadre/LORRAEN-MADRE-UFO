How it works

Press play. Let the hour do the rest.

1

Open the app

Sign in to the Lorraen Madre app. LM Radio sits in the header on every screen.

2

Press play

The station matches the hour. At the top of each hour it glides to the next frequency.

3

Make it yours

Tap Tune to turn the dial, or set your own mix of tone and nature sound.

Features

Small radio. Big calm.

12 hourly stations

A different natural frequency each hour, from 111 Hz to 963 Hz.

Nature mixed in

Birds, creek, rain, ocean, campfire, crickets and more, layered under the tone.

Your mix

Two sliders: how much tone, how much nature.

Ducks for your voice

When you speak a wish, the radio softens so you can hear yourself think.

Background play

Keep it on while you plan, drive, cook or rest.
